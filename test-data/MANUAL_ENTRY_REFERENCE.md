## Notes

- Line numbers vary by year as IRS restructured Form 1040 multiple times (2018, 2019, 2020+)
- Schedule 1 was 1 page in 2018-2020, expanded to 2 pages in 2021+
- For Schedule 1 total adjustments: Line 36 (2018), Line 22 (2019-2020), Line 26 (2021+)
- All values shown with commas for readability; enter without commas in IRS forms
